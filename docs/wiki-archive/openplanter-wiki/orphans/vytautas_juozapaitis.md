# Vytautas Juozapaitis

## Summary
- **Name**: Vytautas Juozapaitis
- **Date of Birth**: 14 December 1963
- **Place of Birth**: Radviliskis, Lithuania
- **Political Party**: Homeland Union (Lietuvos valstiečių ir krikščionys demokratų sąjunga)
- **Position**: Member of the Seimas (2020-2024)

## Professional Background
- **Education**:
  - Graduated from the Lithuanian Academy of Music and Theatre in 1989.
- **Professional Experience**:
  - Soloist at the Lithuanian National Opera and Ballet Theatre since 1990.
  - Soloist at the Kaunas State Musical Theatre since 1989.
  - Collaborated with the Klaipėda State Musical Theatre and appeared in other subsidiary projects.
  - Studied at the European Center for Opera and Voice (ECOV) in Belgium (1992-1993).
  - Studied with Marlena Malas in New York City, USA, and baritone Tom Krause in Finland.
  - Performed in Don Giovanni at the Czech National Opera Theatre in Prague.
  - Touring with his company in Japan (1994-1995) and with the company Teatro Lirico D'Europa in the US (2003).
  - Worked as an opera manager at the Gran Teatre del Liceu in Barcelona, Spain (2005).
  - Teaching vocal performance at the Lithuanian Academy of Music and Theatre since 1996.
  - Professor at the Lithuanian Academy of Music and Theatre and an associate professor at Vilnius College of Higher Education (2006).
  - Opened his personal educational center for culture and art, called "Vytauto Juozapaitio kultūros ir meno centras" (2009).

## Political Career
- **Member of the Seimas**:
  - In office from 16 November 2012 to 12 November 2020.
  - In office from 13 November 2020 to 13 November 2024.
  - Represented the constituency of Aleksotas - Vilijampolė (2020-2024).
  - Preceded by Mindaugas Puidokas.
  - Succeeded by Robertas Kaunas.
