## Matas Skamarakas

### Summary
- **Name**: Matas Skamarakas
- **Date of Birth**: 21 September 1988
- **Place of Birth**: Ariogala, Rajongemeinde Raseiniai, Lithuania
- **Political Party**: Social Democratic Party (Lietuvos socialdemokratų partija)
- **Position**: Member of the Seimas (Incumbent since 26 September 2023)

### Professional Background
- **Education**:
  - Bachelor's degree in Sports Pedagogy from the Lithuanian Sports University (2007-2011)
  - Master's degree in Administration from Kaunas University of Technology (2011-2012)
- **Professional Experience**:
  - Sport teacher at Maironis-Gymnasium in Betygala (2018-2019)
  - Sport teacher at Ariogala Gymnasium (2012-2023)
  - Track and Field Coach at Raseiniai Sports Center (2011-2023)
- **Political Career**:
  - Member of the Raseiniai District Council (2015-2023)
  - Deputy Mayor of Raseiniai (2023)
  - Member of the Seimas (2023-present)
  - Chairman of the Raseiniai branch of the Lithuanian Social Democratic Party (LSDP) (2019-present)
  - Member of the Foreign Affairs Committee of the Seimas

### Family
- **Marital Status**: Married
- **Children**: One son and two daughters
