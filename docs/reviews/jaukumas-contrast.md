## Deliberate exclusions

**Honey (`--attention`) is a surface, never a text colour.** `#C99A4B`
reaches 2.48:1 as text on the linen card, and darkening it far enough
to pass stops it being honey. It is used as a background with
`--attention-foreground` (ink) on top, which measures
5.55:1 light and 8.31:1 dark.

**White on faction colours stays.** Party badges and seat-map chips
set `text-white` over the faction hex. Those are chips, not body
text, and the faction palette is dark enough to carry white at badge
weight.
